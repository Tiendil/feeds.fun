
### Changes

- ff-710 — The "undo read" button moved from the sidebar to the top toolbar.
- ff-711 — Improved Discovery view and toolbar:
  - The Discover Feeds page is now split into clear "Add a feed" and "Import OPML" sections, with handy links to jump between them.
  - New tips explain how to choose feed URLs and avoid duplicate news or wasting tokens and API-key quota.
  - You can now add a feed from the News or Feeds page. The Feeds toolbar also has quick access to importing and exporting OPML files.
  - You can subscribe to a collection right from its public page.
  - Public collection pages now show the collection description with the welcome message and explain why news is always sorted by publication date.
